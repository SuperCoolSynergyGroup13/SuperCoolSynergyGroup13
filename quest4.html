Quest 4 Summary

For this quest we prepared for and visited two organisations in Apeldoorn: Kiwa (Testing, Inspection & Certification) and AgroVision (agricultural software).

Based on the write-ups from: Constantin · Koroush · Leandro · Niels · Serhii.

About the companies

AgroVision B.V.

Dutch software company (founded 1986, Apeldoorn) building tools for agriculture: livestock & dairy management, crops, finance, and feed formulation. Active in 40+ countries with ~200 staff (growth via the 2024 AgriSyst acquisition), serving about 19,000 customers.

  • Products: pig & dairy tools (e.g., CowVision), crop registration & compliance, farm accounting, feed formulas
  • Focus: farmer-centric, data-driven, moving legacy apps to modern cloud platforms
  • Values: Adaptive · Future-proof · Farmer’s mentality · Knowledge-driven
  • Group: Part of the Kiwa Group
Cloud Data Agritech

Kiwa

Global organisation in Testing, Inspection & Certification. Started in 1948 safeguarding drinking water; now active across construction, energy, water, food, agriculture, health and environment with international labs and experts.

  • Mission: create trust through safety, quality and sustainability
  • Known for: the Kiwa Quality Mark (tested to official standards)
  • CSR: CO₂ reduction, employee wellbeing, and UN SDGs
  • Scale (2023): revenue ≈ €1,339.2 million

Key points
  • Reliability first: AI-generated code isn’t production-ready by default; quality and security come first.
  • Cybersecurity is everyday work: AgroVision faces attacks daily; monitoring and backups matter.
  • Certification takes time: trust builds over years via testing and audits.
  • Cloud transition: moving desktop systems to accessible cloud solutions.
  • Values: farmer-centric design and evidence-based decisions.
  • Workflows: Agile/Scrum and DevOps link class practice to industry reality.
  • Internship readiness: soft skills plus C#, TypeScript, Angular.
  • Context: Kiwa builds trust through standards; AgroVision enables data-driven farming.
