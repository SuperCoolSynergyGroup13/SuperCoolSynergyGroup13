Garbage in, Growth out

When I first started using AI, I thought it was an easy way to finish my work. I could just ask a question, get the answer, and move on. It felt fast and smart, but after a while, I realized I wasn’t learning much.

I was letting AI do the hard part for me. And honestly, that made me feel a bit empty.

Then one day, I asked myself: “Why am I using AI like this?” That’s when things started to change.

Now, I use AI as a helper, not a shortcut. When I’m stuck, it gives me ideas. When I don’t know where to start, it helps me begin. But I make sure that the real thinking still comes from me.

I’ve learned that the answers I get depend on the questions I ask. If I ask something unclear, I get unclear answers. But when I take time to ask good questions, AI gives much better help.

Sometimes I still use it too much. Like when I open it before even trying to solve something myself. When that happens, I stop and remind myself: “Try first, even if you make mistakes.” Because mistakes are how I learn.

So now, I try to use AI wisely. It’s not about finishing work quickly, it’s about learning something new each time.

If I could give one piece of advice to my future self, it would be this: Use AI with purpose. Think for yourself. And remember, learning is the real goal.
